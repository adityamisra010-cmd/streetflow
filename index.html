What we do

Four ways to move with us

Whichever door you come through, it's the same crew, the same standard.

Dance Classes

Explore diverse dance styles, from folk to street and technical. Learn choreography from beginner to advanced levels. Multiple batches across Pune, online and to your doorstep.

Custom Sangeet nights, baraat sets and event entries, prepared from scratch at your convenience to perfection. Expert choreography for corporate events, festivals and special occasions.

Fitness Sessions

Online Batches

Live online dance and fitness classes you can join from anywhere in the world. Same coaching, delivered straight to your room with flexible timings.

About us

What is Street Flow?

We are a community of passionate dancers dedicated to creating a positive, welcoming space where people of all ages and skill levels can learn, grow, and thrive together. At Street Flow, we believe dance is more than just movement. It's a way to express, connect, and grow.

Through every class, we aim to inspire confidence, creativity, discipline, and a lifelong love for dance. Whether you're dancing for a performance, fitness, fun, self-expression, or to pursue your passion, we focus on building strong fundamentals while ensuring every class is engaging, energetic, and enjoyable.
