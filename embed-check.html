Which reels will play inside the website?

Instagram decides this, not the site. A reel with licensed music (or one that is restricted) refuses to embed, and its player sends visitors to instagram.com instead. This page loads all six the exact same way the website does, so you can see which ones actually play.

  2. Press play inside each one.
